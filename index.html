How booking works

No back-and-forth texting. Everything happens online.

  1. Pick a service and time

    See real availability and choose the slot that works for you.

  2. Pay your deposit

    A deposit secures your spot and goes toward your total.

  3. Get your reminder

    You'll receive a text and email reminder before your appointment.

Policies

Please read before booking.

Deposits
A non-refundable $15 deposit is required to book and is applied to your total.
Rescheduling
Reschedule at least 48 hours before your appointment and your deposit transfers to the new date.
Late arrivals
Arriving more than 15 minutes late may shorten your service or require rescheduling.
Come prepared
Arrive with a clean, moisturized face. Share any skin sensitivities when booking.
