Meet the artist

Makeup that looks like you, glowing

Lafiglow was created for the moments that matter: the wedding morning, the birthday dinner, the graduation photos, the night out you have been planning for weeks. My goal is simple. You should look in the mirror and see yourself at your most radiant, not a mask.

“Skin first, glow always, and a look that lasts from the first photo to the last dance.”

Every appointment starts with a conversation about your style, your skin and the occasion. From there I build a look that photographs beautifully and still feels like you in person. I work with all skin tones and types, use professional, hygienic products, and welcome you to the studio or come to you anywhere in the Quad Cities.

  • All skin tones
  • Long-wear finish
  • Clean, sanitized kit
  • Studio or on-location

Good to know

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Email hello [at] lafiglow.com or send a DM on Instagram.

Service area

Proudly serving the Quad Cities: Davenport, Bettendorf, Moline, Rock Island, East Moline and nearby. Traveling farther? Reach out and we'll make it work.

How far in advance should I book?

For events, 2 to 4 weeks ahead is ideal. For weddings, book 3 to 6 months out, especially for spring and fall dates, and schedule your trial 1 to 2 months before the big day.

Do you come to my home or venue?

Yes. Choose "come to you" when you book and I'll travel to your home, venue or hotel anywhere in the Quad Cities for a $30 travel fee. Prefer the studio? Pick the studio option instead.

How long will my makeup last?

Looks are built with long-wear products and set to last all day and night. For weddings and long events, I can include a small touch-up tip so you stay fresh.

I have sensitive skin or allergies. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Please note any sensitivities or allergies when booking so I can plan the right products for you.

How should I prepare?

Arrive with a clean, moisturized face and no makeup. Bring inspiration photos if you have them. If you wear contacts, put them in before your appointment. Once you've booked, please fill out the short pre-appointment form so I can plan products for your skin.

Can you do my whole bridal party?

Yes. Bridal party makeup is $50 per person, booked as one appointment per person. For larger groups or very early start times, email me so we can plan the schedule together.

Before you book

Policies

A few simple rules that keep every appointment smooth and on time.

A non-refundable $15 deposit is required to book and is applied to your total.

Rescheduling

Late arrivals

Arriving more than 15 minutes late may shorten your service or require rescheduling.

Come prepared

Arrive with a clean, moisturized face. Share any skin sensitivities when booking.
